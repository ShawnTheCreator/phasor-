## 3. Production & Recording Checklist

- [ ] **Voiceover:** Speak with calm authority, clear enunciation, and natural cadence. Do not rush.
- [ ] **Audio Mix:** Low ambient cinematic synth swell in the background; duck music volume by -12 dB during speech so the voice is crystal clear.
- [ ] **Resolution:** 1080p (1920x1080) at 60fps or 30fps MP4.
- [ ] **Duration Check:** Total file duration **must be $\le 30.0$ seconds** (e.g., 29.8s). Do not submit a 31-second video.
- [ ] **Captions:** Add crisp English subtitles across the bottom third for judges reviewing on mute.
